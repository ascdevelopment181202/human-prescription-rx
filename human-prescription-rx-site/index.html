Dr. Pragnya

Biography

Dr. Pragnya is an ophthalmologist, educator, and podcast host with a passion for understanding what drives human health, performance, and fulfilment. Having completed her specialist ophthalmology training in Australia and recently attaining her FRANZCO qualification, she is currently undertaking fellowships in refractive surgery at London Vision Clinic and Moorfields Eye Hospital in London.

Alongside her clinical work, she has a longstanding interest in psychology, wellbeing, leadership, and the cultural factors that shape health and human potential. She serves on the boards of RANZCO Women in Ophthalmology and the Cultural Curriculum Committee, advocating for greater diversity, wellbeing, and positive change within medicine.

As the host of Human Prescription Rx, she brings together her background in medicine with a curiosity about the mind–body connection, human behaviour, and personal growth — exploring the science and stories behind living well.
